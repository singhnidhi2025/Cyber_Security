# a) SecuDream(tm). Cyber security course ... of my dreams..
## Goals:
After the course , the students will be able to:
- Understand Cyber security risks, their impact on businesses and ways to mitigate them.
- Interpret technical cybersecurity reports and make informed choices aligned to business goals.
- make effective communication regarding cyber risks to executives/stakeholders and take part in stategic decision making.
## Course Outline/Agenda:

|**Session**|**Theme**                                      |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------|
|  1        |Cybersecurity Fundamentals for managers        |
|  2        |Understanding Threats and Vulberabilities      |
|  3        |Risk assesment & Risk Prioritization Models    |
|  4        |Communicating Risk to Executives               |
|  5        |Cyber Governance ,Culture & Strategic Alignment|

## Exercises:

-Session 1: Interactive Discussions on scenarios;light hands-on lab analyzing simplofied alerts ofr phishing examples.
-Session 2: Case studies discussion;simplified threat dashboards;roleplay:managers receiving briefings from technical teams.
-Session 3: Risk matrix exercises;group discussions;optional roleplay as technical/management teams.
-Session 4: Boardroom roleplay presenting risk reports;executive friendly brief exercises;reflection on communication effectiveness.
-Session 4: Case study discussions;group exercise designing a mini governance plan;reflection on culture and strategic alignment.

## Frameworks:

**1. Risk Matrix/Heat Map**- For prioortizing threats,making informed decisions,and visualizing risk for stakeholders.
**2.NIST Cybersecurity Framework(Manager Version)**: Provides a structured ,strategic view of cybersecurity aligned with business processes.

## Visiting Lectures,Excursions & Special Events:

- **Lecturers:** Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) from bog organizations, corporate governance experts.
- **Excursions:** SOC tours, simulated boardroom meetings.
- **Special Events:** Capture-the-Flag lite exercises,incident decisionhackathons,executive panel discussions.

## Rationale:
SecuDream(tm) is designed for non-technical managers who need to make strategic,risk-based decisions. The course build from foundational knowledge to threat awareness,risk prioritization,executive communication and governance.
- Exercises combine **discussions,hands-on-lab and roelplay**to make learning practical and engaging.
- Frameworks are choosen to **simplify risk assesment** and link cybersecurity to business strategy.


  This course ensures managers leave **confident in inetrpreting technical reports,assessing business impact, prioritizing risks,and influencing strategy**, preparing them to lead effectively in cyber-aware organizations.
